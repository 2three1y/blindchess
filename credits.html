What Blind Chess is built from

I made Blind Chess (Carter, 2three1y), and it is MIT licensed. I built it only from repos I starred on GitHub, plus chess.js, the rules library the starred chess projects depend on.

  • License: MIT
  • Starred: yes, checked Oct 23, 2026
