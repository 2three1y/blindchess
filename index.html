    Help

    Typing moves

    • Chess notation: e4, Nf3, Bxc6, O-O, e8=Q. Capital letters are optional.
    • From and to squares: e2 e4, or g1 to f3.
    • Words: knight to f3, pawn takes d5, castle kingside, castle queenside.

    Commands you can type

    • my pieces, or their pieces
    • where is my queen, where are my knights
    • what's on e5, or just e5?
    • moves from g1, or legal moves
    • last move, history, status, threats, material, read board
    • repeat, undo, resign, help

    Keys on the board

    • Arrow keys move between squares. Home and End go to the start and end of a rank.
    • Enter or Space picks up a piece or drops it.
    • Escape puts the piece back.
    • Question mark: what attacks this square. M: where this piece can go.
    • P: my pieces. O: their pieces. L: last move. T: whose turn.
    • Slash jumps to the move box.

    Sounds

    • A soft drop: a move. A glassy hit: a capture. A bong: check.
    • A rising chime: it's your turn.
